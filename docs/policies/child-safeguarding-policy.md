# [Club name] child safeguarding policy

Version [version] · [date]

[Club name] is committed to the safety and wellbeing of every child and young
person in the club. This policy adopts [the governing body's member protection
and child safeguarding policies] and says how the club applies them.

## Our commitments

- We put children's safety first in every decision.
- We listen to children and act on what they tell us.
- We screen and train the adults who work with children.
- We keep parents and guardians informed and involved.
- We report child abuse and serious crime to Police and the governing body
  straight away. We never try to deal with it inside the club.

## Who is responsible

- **Child Safety Officer:** [name, phone, email].
- **Complaints Manager:** [name, phone, email].
- The committee is responsible for this policy and reviews it every year.

Both are named in the app, and shown to everyone on **Raise a concern**.

## Screening

Everyone in child-related work at the club holds a current Working With
Children Check, or a valid exemption (in WA, for example, a parent volunteering
at their own child's club, or a volunteer under 18). The app pauses a person's
role the day their check expires, and admins can stand someone down straight
away.

## How we communicate with children

- No private messages. Messages go to whole squads, teams or roles, and a
  child's parents or guardians receive a copy of every message to their child.
- Adults don't contact children through personal phones or social media.
- [Add rules for photos, travel, changing rooms, transport and overnight trips.]

## Children's information

See the club's privacy policy. A child's check-ins and injuries are seen by
their coaches, physios and the committee only after a verified parent or
guardian consents in the app.

## Raising a concern

Anyone can raise a concern in the app, from the top of every tab, the Me tab,
the check-in screen, any injury or any message, and can ask for their name not to be shown.
It goes to the Child Safety Officer, the Complaints Manager and admins, never
to the person it's about. You can also contact them directly, or:

- Police: **000** if a child is in danger now.
- Kids Helpline: **1800 55 1800**.
- Safe Sport Hotline (Sport Integrity Australia): **1800 161 361**.
- [Governing body name and complaints contact].

## How we respond

1. Make the child safe. Call 000 if they are in danger.
2. Record the concern in the app's concerns register.
3. Refer child abuse and serious crime to Police and [governing body] straight
   away, and record the referral.
4. Support the child and their family, and keep the concern confidential.
5. Stand the person down while it's looked into, where that's needed.
6. Record every step and close the concern with a note saying why.

## Review

The committee reviews this policy every year, and after any serious incident,
and runs [the governing body's child-safe self-assessment].
