# [Club name] privacy policy

Version [version] · [date]

This policy explains what [Club name] ("the club") keeps about its members,
including children, in the club app, who can see it, and how long it's kept.

## Who we are

[Club name], [address]. Our privacy contact is [name, role, email]. Questions
about this policy, or requests to see or correct your information, go to them.

## What we keep

| Information | About whom | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, preferred name, email, role, squads and teams | Every member | To run the club and send club messages |
| Date of birth | Athletes and volunteers | To know who is under 18 and apply the rules for children |
| Working With Children Check number and expiry, or exemption | Coaches, managers, physios, committee members and admins | The law requires people working with children to be screened |
| Parent and guardian links | Athletes under 18 and their parents or guardians | So parents see what the club holds about their child |
| Daily check-ins (sleep, soreness, stress, mood, notes) | Athletes | To look after athletes' wellbeing |
| Injuries, physio updates and medical letters or reports | Athletes | To keep injured athletes safe and clear them to play |
| Game goals and reflections | Athletes | Coaching |
| Attendance | Athletes | To run training and selections |
| Messages | Everyone who sends or receives them | Club communication |
| Integrity training certificates | Members | The sport requires the training |
| Concerns raised | Whoever raises one, and whoever it's about | To keep children safe |
| Consents given by parents and guardians | Athletes under 18 | To record what parents agreed to |

## Who sees it

[Copy the "Who can see what" table from section 11 of the app's user guide, so
this matches what the app does.]

For athletes under 18, coaches, physios and the committee see the child's
daily check-ins and injuries only after a verified parent or guardian gives
consent in the app. Parents and guardians can withdraw consent at any time,
in the app. Parents' access ends on their child's 18th birthday.

Photos of children's injuries are [not accepted / accepted only from a verified
parent or guardian who consents to each one]. Every view and share of an
injury document is recorded and shown to the athlete, their parents and admins.

Information isn't shared outside the club, except: with Police or the sport's
governing body when a child's safety requires it; with the Working with
Children Screening Unit to check a card; or where the law requires.

## Your choices

You can see and correct your details in the app under **Your details**. To ask
for a copy of what we hold, or to delete your account, contact [privacy
contact]. Some records, such as safeguarding concerns and screening checks,
may need to be kept even after you leave.

## Complaints

Contact [privacy contact] first. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the
Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

> Note for clubs: whether the Privacy Act applies to your club depends on its
> turnover and whether it is a health service provider because it keeps injury
> records. Get advice and adjust this policy to match.
