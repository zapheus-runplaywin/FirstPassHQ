This policy explains what [Club name] ("the club") keeps about its members,
including children, in the club app, who can see it, and how long it's kept.

## What we keep

| Information | About whom | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, preferred name, email, role, squads and teams | Every member | To run the club and send club messages |
| Date of birth | Athletes and volunteers | To know who is under 18 and apply the rules for children |
| Working With Children Check number and expiry, or exemption | Coaches, managers, physios, committee members and admins | The law requires people working with children to be screened |

| Messages | Everyone who sends or receives them | Club communication |
| Integrity training certificates | Members | The sport requires the training |
| Concerns raised | Whoever raises one, and whoever it's about | To keep children safe |
| Consents given by parents and guardians | Athletes under 18 | To record what parents agreed to |
| Who opened a child's records, and when | Athletes under 18, and the club people who opened them | So parents can see who looked at their child's information |
| Code of conduct acceptances, if the club asks for them in the app | Members, and parents accepting for their child | To record who agreed to the code of conduct each season |

## Who sees it

[Copy the "Who can see what" table from section 11 of the app's user guide, so
this matches what the app does.]

For athletes under 18, coaches, physios and the committee see the child's
daily check-ins and injuries only after a verified parent or guardian gives
consent in the app. Parents and guardians can withdraw consent at any time,
in the app. Parents' access ends on their child's 18th birthday.

Photos of children's injuries are [not accepted / accepted only from a verified
parent or guardian who consents to each one]. Every view and share of an
injury document is recorded and shown to the athlete, their parents and admins.

Information isn't shared outside the club, except: with Police or the sport's
governing body when a child's safety requires it; with the Working with
Children Screening Unit to check a card; or where the law requires.

## What the app doesn't do

The app is for running the club, not social media. Members can't message each
other privately or reply to messages, and there are no feeds, profile
pictures, likes or following. Messages go to whole squads, teams or roles, and
a parent or guardian gets a copy of every message to or from their child.

## Where it's kept

The app keeps the club's information in its own database, separate from every
other club, hosted by [hosting provider] in [location]. Only the people above
can see it, and only through the app.

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report to FirstPass, the app's maker,
through Sentry, which stores it in the European Union for 30 days. A report
says what went wrong in the app and on what kind of phone. Names, emails,
dates of birth, member ids, messages and anything else members or the club
entered are taken out on the phone before it is sent, so a report can't say
who it came from.

## How long we keep it

The app removes old records each night once the club has switched this on.
These are the app's standard periods; the club can set longer ones in the app
(update this list to match the club's settings).

- Injury records: 12 months after the athlete leaves the club.
- Injury documents (letters, reports and photos): [90 days] after the injury is cleared, or when
  the season is archived if that's sooner. The record of each document, who added it and who
  viewed it stays with the injury.
- Daily check-ins: [24 months] from the day of the check-in.
- Game goals, reflections and coaches' comments: [24 months] after the game.
- Message text: [24 months] after it was sent. A record that the message was
  sent, who it went to and who read it is kept, so a complaint can still be
  looked into.
- Attendance and reported absences: [7 years], as the record of who was with which children.
- Integrity training certificates: [7 years] after they were added.
- The record of who opened a child's records: [7 years].
- Concerns and what was done about them: never removed by the app.
- Other information, such as your details and Working With Children Check
  records: while you are a member, then [period].

## Your choices

You can see and correct your details in the app under **Your details**. To ask
for a copy of what we hold, or to delete your account, contact [privacy
contact]. Some records, such as safeguarding concerns and screening checks,
may need to be kept even after you leave.

Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
