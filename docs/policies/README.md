# Policy templates

Plain-English starting points for the three policies the app links to under
**Club > Safety and conduct > Safeguarding**. Each club adapts them, publishes them on its
own website, and pastes the links into the app. They are templates, not legal
advice: have your committee review them, and get advice where the notes say so.

- [Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md): what the club keeps about members,
  especially children, who sees it and for how long.
- [Child safeguarding policy](child-safeguarding-policy.md): how the club keeps
  children safe and handles concerns.
- [Code of conduct](code-of-conduct.md): what the club expects of coaches,
  volunteers, parents and players.

Replace everything in [square brackets]. When you change a policy, change the
**Policy version** in the app too, so each parent's consent is recorded against
the version they read.

The sport's governing bodies publish their own policies, which bind affiliated
clubs. Read these templates alongside them; where they differ, the governing
body's policy applies.
