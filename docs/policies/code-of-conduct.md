# [Club name] code of conduct

Version [version] · [date]

This code applies to everyone in the club: players, coaches, managers,
physios, officials, committee members, volunteers, parents and guardians. It
sits alongside [the governing body's code of behaviour], which also applies.

## Everyone

- Treat everyone with respect and fairness, whatever their age, ability,
  background, gender, sexuality, culture or religion.
- Put children's safety and wellbeing first.
- No bullying, harassment, discrimination or abuse, in person or online.
- No violence, threats or abusive language.
- Respect umpires' and officials' decisions.
- No alcohol, smoking or vaping around junior players, and no drugs.
- Raise a concern when something isn't right (see the safeguarding policy).

## Coaches, managers, physios and volunteers

- Hold a current Working With Children Check, or a valid exemption, and
  complete the training the club asks for.
- Never be alone with a child out of sight of others. Don't contact children
  privately, by phone, text or social media. Use the club app, where parents
  are copied.
- Don't give children lifts, gifts or special attention without their
  parents' knowledge and the club's agreement.
- Keep players' information private. Don't take, keep or share photos of
  children on personal devices.
- Report any concern about a child's safety to the Child Safety Officer.

## Parents and guardians

- Support your child and their team positively, win or lose.
- Leave coaching to the coaches and decisions to the umpires.
- Keep your contact details current and read the club's messages.
- Tell the club about anything that affects your child's safety.

## Players

- Play fairly and follow the rules.
- Respect your teammates, opponents, coaches and umpires.
- Tell a parent, a coach or the Child Safety Officer if you, or someone else,
  is not OK. You can also raise a concern in the app without your name.

## If the code is broken

The committee may give a warning, suspend someone from activities, or end
their membership, following [the governing body's complaints and disciplinary
process]. Anything involving a child's safety is handled under the child
safeguarding policy.
