# Google Play: store listing

Play Console > FirstPass HQ > Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing. Every feature named
here is merged and described in the [user guide](../user-guide.md). Screenshots and the feature
graphic come in step 3 (Fri 4 Dec), in `docs/store/screenshots/`.

## App details

**App name** (30 characters at most): **FirstPass HQ** (12). **To confirm** if you want another
name.

**Short description** (80 at most):

> Run your netball club: messages, attendance, injuries and child safety. (71)

**Full description** (4,000 at most):

> FirstPass HQ is the club administration app for netball clubs. Admins, coaches, managers,
> physios, the committee, athletes and their parents each see what their role needs, and nothing
> more.
>
> Each club has its own private database. Open the app, enter your club's code, sign in, and ask
> to join. A club admin approves you and puts you in your squad or team.
>
> FOR COACHES AND MANAGERS
> • Messages to your squad or team, with read receipts
> • Attendance at training and games, and absences athletes report ahead of time
> • Injured athletes in your team and their current restrictions
> • Session plans, game goals and athletes' training load
> • Team chat groups with your managers, captains and physios, if your club turns them on
>
> FOR ATHLETES AND PARENTS
> • The club calendar and your team's events on the home screen
> • A to-do list: check-ins, surveys, clearances and training to complete
> • Report an injury, upload the clearance letter and follow it to return to play
> • Report an absence before the session
>
> FOR ADMINS AND THE COMMITTEE
> • Approve members, check them against PlayHQ and assign squads and teams
> • Import fixtures from PlayHQ into the club calendar
> • Working With Children Check records, exemptions and reminders
> • Messages to the whole club or to chosen roles, squads and teams
> • Surveys, awards, integrity training and governance reports
> • Choose how long the club keeps each kind of record
>
> BUILT FOR CHILD SAFETY
> • Athletes under 18 stay locked until a verified parent or guardian is linked
> • Parents get a copy of every message to or from their child
> • No direct messages, feeds, profile pictures, likes or following
> • Chat groups only if your club turns them on. Under 18s post only in their own team's
>   coach-led group, coaches can hide any post, and parents get a copy of every post
> • Parents see who opened their child's records
> • Anyone can raise a concern with the club's Child Safety Officer
>
> No ads, no tracking. Each club's data is kept in its own database in Australia.
>
> FirstPass HQ needs a club code from your club. To bring FirstPass HQ to your club, contact us
> through the support page.

The full description is about 2,100 characters. Google doesn't allow emoji or "best", "#1" or
price claims in these fields; the text has none.
