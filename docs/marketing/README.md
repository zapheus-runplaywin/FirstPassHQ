# Marketing documents

Sales material for showing FirstPass HQ to clubs, built from the app at build 301 (14 October 2026). For how to use the app, see the [user guide](../user-guide.md).

**Brochures** (12 pages) are the short version, for a club president and committee weighing up the app, and for the coaches, athletes and parents on the participation side.

**Detailed guides** (37 pages) are the companion to read after the brochure, for a club that wants the detail. They don't repeat the brochure. They open with each claim the brochure makes and the evidence behind it, then take the app part by part for the people who use it: the president and committee, child safety, coaches, athletes and athlete advocates, parents and guardians, and the admins who run the club. Each feature has two screens and an evidence note. They close with "who sees what" and what a club sets up. Optional features are marked, and the Working With Children Check rules shown are Western Australia's.
