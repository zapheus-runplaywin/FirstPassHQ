# Committee guide audit — 17 October 2026

Scope: the committee role guide (`docs/user-guide.md` section 8 in full, the Committee row and column of the section 1 tables, every section 11 row that names or omits the committee, and section 12 "Raising a concern" and "For committees, boards and admins"), plus the sections the committee PDF shares with other roles (2 Getting started, 3 Things everyone can do, and the parts of sections 6 and 9 the committee section points to: Sending messages, Taking attendance, Attendance records, Season KPIs, Integrity training, The concerns register, Safeguarding settings) read as a committee member would read them. Every claim was checked against main at `d355281` plus PR #143's branch: screens in `src/app`, logic in `src/lib`, and the database rules (RLS and functions) in `supabase/migrations`, always the last definition of each function. Who-sees claims were checked against the database rule, not only the screen. `is_committee()` means committee or admin throughout; `has_role('committee')` is the paused-aware check used by the app and by every committee function.

**Result:** the committee guide is largely accurate: the Committee column of the feature table is right cell by cell with one exception (A6), every section 11 row that names the committee matches the database, and the pause rules, committee checks date, certificate checking, attendance, KPIs and messaging all work as described. The audit found four wrong statements about injuries and concerns (A1 to A4), one safeguarding gap in the database (A5, a committee member can send back their own child's clearance request), a few overclaims (A6 to A9), a handful of stale in-app texts and small bugs (B), about fifteen wording slips (C) and a long list of things a committee member can do that section 8 never mentions (D). All four committee screenshots are still accurate.

## C. Wording slips (guide only)

1. Section 8 intro: say that a committee member who also coaches, manages or physios can't be exempted with a reason (`canBeExempt`, `src/lib/safety.ts:21-26`); the role table in section 1 says "unless an admin exempts them" without the limit. Section 10 has it.
2. Section 8 "Committee checks date": the setting is labelled "Committee checks required from" under Safeguarding; use the label once.
3. Section 8 "Until then their home screen says when the role will pause": add that an admin's to-do counts such a member as "missing" from day one, not from the date (`admin_safety_todos`).
4. Section 8 "Messages": a committee member who is also a captain or leads a group gets the Everyone / A squad or team chooser; "everyone in the club" excludes guests, temporary helpers and view-only physios unless the club setting gives physios club messages. Section 1 and 11 say this, section 8 doesn't.
5. Section 8 "Check-ins": the flagged count is hidden together with the scores; the "within 4 check-ins" rule compares distinct athletes over the period and hides only when the other group has 5 or more and the gap is 1 to 4 (equal counts aren't hidden).
6. Section 8 "injuries": the committee also sees injuries of athletes who have left, under Including cleared, for 12 months (section 11 says so, section 8 doesn't); the sign-off to-do also goes to admins.
7. Section 8 "Share": on the web, Share opens the document in a new tab rather than the share sheet (`src/lib/injuries.ts:211-214`).
8. Section 8 "every committee member and admin gets a to-do" for certificates: only those who can review it (never the uploader or their guardian), and only when the Integrity training feature is on; the to-do reads "Check N training certificates — Approve them, or send them back with a note".
9. Section 8 "Everyone's progress": one season at a time (season chips appear only with two or more seasons), archived courses listed too; the filters are Everyone, Still to do, Waiting, Approved.
10. Section 8 "attendance for any whole squad or the whole club": the committee can also take attendance for a team in a club without squads ("Another team" in the picker), and can change a whole-club session anyone took or a session they took themselves, but not another person's squad session (`can_edit_attendance_session`).
11. Section 8 "Season KPIs": committee members can also edit or remove a KPI the head coach set (`kpi_can_edit` ignores who set it), and a course or review point shows "Set by" / "Updated by"; never for yourself.
12. Section 8 "Write their reviews": the "Write {name}'s KPI review" to-do appears only for people whose KPIs you set at least one of.
13. Section 1 feature table, "See injuries: All": under-18s without injuries consent are not listed at all for a non-admin committee member; "All*" with the footnote, or leave as section 8 explains it.
14. Section 1 feature table, "Sign off injury clearances: Accept": the committee can also **send back** a request, including one no coach or physio has endorsed yet; "Accept or send back".
15. Section 11 "Your profile and squads: you, the people in your squads and teams, and admins": the committee (and admins) also see every athlete's name on the whole-club register, attendance histories and absences, every coach's and manager's name under Season KPIs, every member listed under a training course, and every injured athlete's name. Names, not profiles.
16. Section 12 "Coaches, managers, physios, committee members and admins need a current Working With Children Check before they're approved": committee members and admins can be exempted with a reason instead, and the rule applies to new committee members, not those approved before checks were needed. Sections 1 and 8 say this correctly.
17. Section 12 "Stand-downs … statistician … roles stop": see B13.
18. Section 12 "declarations not yet accepted" are counted inside the "N missing or expired" WWC to-do, not a to-do of their own; "turn 18 this month" means the next 30 days.
19. Section 12 "The concerns register lists every concern": the PDF leaves out any concern about the person exporting it (its own footnote says so).
20. Section 3 awards "you only see and vote for members of your own squad or team": a committee member votes in **every** group's slot of a per-group award whose voter roles name the committee (the admin screen says "Committee and admins vote wherever they are"). Section 8 never mentions awards.
21. Section 1 "Complete integrity training: applies to the roles, squads and teams an admin chooses": a course limited to particular squads or teams never reaches committee members, even when it names the committee, because nobody is in a group as "committee". Only all-groups courses do (the admin screen says so).
22. Section 8 "Concerns": the card is "Concerns — The concerns register" / "N new concerns to look at", and the to-do "Look at N new concerns — Refer child abuse and serious crimes to Police straight away". There is no push notification for a new concern.

## D. Things a committee member can do that the guide doesn't mention

Worth adding to section 8:
- **Joining:** Committee is one of the "I am a" choices; no squad or team, card number or title is asked for at join. The title is added later under Your details (placeholder "President, Secretary, Treasurer…", up to 40 characters) or by an admin on your member page.
- **Header with several roles:** "Coach, Committee · President · Head Coach, Open A", "Committee, Guardian · Treasurer". While the committee role is paused the title disappears from the header (it is kept) and the card reads "Committee paused".
- **While paused:** Messages (committee-only members become read-nothing), Check-ins, Injuries and clearance to-dos, Attendance, Season KPIs, certificate checking and Everyone's progress, the committee calendar, club-wide messaging and the Title field all go. The code of conduct to-do, your own integrity training, award votes, personal tasks, Raise a concern and Coming up stay.
- **Being an officer:** any cleared committee member can be named Child Safety Officer, Complaints Manager or WWC Registration Officer by an admin. Officers get the Concerns card: the register with Who it's about search, Add note, Referred to {body}, Referred to Police, Being looked into, Closed (with "Write why it's closed"), the reporter's contact preference unless anonymous, "This concern isn't available to you" for one about themselves, a limit of 10 concerns raised per day, and Export the register as a PDF.
- **Check-ins:** Day by day and Trends chips, Today and Yesterday, 2 weeks / 4 weeks / 3 months (default 4 weeks), tap a day, "Not enough check-ins yet", Whole club first then squads and teams without a squad; no check-in to-do for the committee.
- **Messages:** Inbox and Sent chips, "Read by X of Y" and the receipts list, 4,000 characters, the "Report this message" link; a committee member who is also a captain gets the group chooser.
- **Injuries:** Current / Including cleared chip, headings "Waiting for your sign-off", "Injured athletes", "Your injuries", "Your athletes"; the badge "N waiting for you"; the to-do "Review {name}'s clearance" / "Review N injury clearances"; View on a document at any status until it's removed ("Removed {date}, after the club's keep period. The record of it stays."); accepting says "This clears the athlete to train and play. Check the proof in the history below first." with the button "Accept and clear to play"; a send-back note is mandatory ("Say why it's being sent back."); opening a child's injury page is recorded (one record per 30 minutes), document views always.
- **Integrity training:** the Check certificates card ("Nothing waiting for you" / "N certificates waiting for you"), the badge "N certificates to check", the review list oldest first with the uploader's note, "What needs fixing?" with a 1,000-character note, opening any member's certificate from the course list, Export CSV (Name, Status, Completed on, Checked by, Checked on); the member sees the reviewer's name with a send-back note.
- **Attendance:** the register's Away and Injured notes, the duplicate-session warning, the Athletes tab with periods and per-athlete history, "Away in the next 4 weeks" for every athlete with reason, details and who reported it.
- **Season KPIs:** recording progress for a coach or manager, "Remove" on a coach's own view text, "No review dates have been set" text, past seasons read-only, opening an under-18 coach's sheet is recorded.
- **Committee calendar:** its own colour and filter, named "Committee calendar"; a paused committee member doesn't see it.
- **Code of conduct each season** applies to the committee ("Accept the code of conduct — Every member accepts it each season").
- **Delete account:** certificates you approved keep your name as the checker; any officer position you held is emptied (A8).
