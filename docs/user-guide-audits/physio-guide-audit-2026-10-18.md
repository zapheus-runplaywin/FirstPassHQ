# Physio guide audit — 18 October 2026

Scope: the physio role guide (`docs/user-guide.md` section 9 intro and "Physios", the Physio row and column of the section 1 tables, section 6 "Injuries and clearances" and "Volunteer exemptions" as a physio reads them, every section 11 row that names or omits physios, and section 12 "Raising a concern", "Who counts as a child", "Injury documents and photos" and "For coaches, managers, physios and volunteers"), plus sections 2 and 3 read as a physio. Every claim was checked against PR #143's branch at `f6367fc`: screens in `src/app`, logic in `src/lib`, and the database rules in `supabase/migrations`, always the last definition of each function. Who-sees claims were checked against the database rule, not only the screen. `physio_looks_after(athlete)` (Head Physio, or placed in a current squad or team holding the athlete) is the rule behind every physio claim.

**Result:** no physio claim in the guide is flatly wrong against the database. The Physio column of the feature table is right cell by cell, the three placement cases, the endorsement rules, consent gate, check-in averages and record views all match. What the audit found is one wrong hint in the app that contradicts the guide (A1, the admin member page still describes the old "every athlete if in none" rule), two guide overclaims about what the database closes and who votes (A2, A3), one gap between physios and coaches after the squad phase (A4), a records gap on account deletion (A5), a possible crash in the inbox (B2), stale "coach" wording where a physio endorses (fixed on this PR with the committee audit), and two member-page screenshots that are out of date.

## C. Wording slips (guide only)

1. Section 9: say that a physio in no group sees "You are not in a squad yet." under My squads and teams, and that while paused the card reads "Physio paused" with the renew-your-check text, "Physio (paused)" in the header.
2. Section 9: check-in averages and trends cover every squad and team in the club, whatever the physio is placed in; the home card reads "X of Y athletes checked in today".
3. Section 9: the roster shows the Head Physio as "Physio"; "Head Physio" appears on the home screen header and the Members list (section 10 says this).
4. Section 9: list the Injuries screen headings "Waiting for your sign-off" and "Injured athletes", the "N current injuries · N waiting for you" card, and the to-do "Review {name}'s clearance".
5. Section 3, Messages: a physio's messages are labelled "To Physios", "To {squad}" or "To Everyone in the club".
6. Section 6, exemption to-do: the to-do reads "Accept the exemption declaration — Your club role starts once you do".

## D. Things a physio can do that the guide doesn't mention

- Raise a concern and be named in one (section 3 covers it); integrity training and the code of conduct apply to physios (tables say so).
- Opening an under-18's injury is recorded with the role "Physio"; document views the same.
- A physio never gets Share; documents open in the app only.
