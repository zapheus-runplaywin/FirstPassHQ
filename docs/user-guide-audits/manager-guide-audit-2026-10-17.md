# Manager guide audit — 17 October 2026

Scope: the manager role guide (`docs/user-guide.md` section 7 in full, the Manager row and column of the section 1 tables, every section 11 row that names or omits managers, and the section 12 parts "Who counts as a child" and "For coaches, managers, physios and volunteers"), plus the sections the manager PDF shares with other roles (2 Getting started, 3 Things everyone can do, and the parts of section 6 the manager section points to: Sending messages, Taking attendance, Attendance records, Season KPIs) read as a manager would read them. Every claim was checked against main at `d355281` (after PR #142): screens in `src/app`, logic in `src/lib`, and the database rules (RLS and functions) in `supabase/migrations`, always the last definition of each function. Who-sees claims were checked against the database rule, not only the screen.

**Result:** the manager guide is accurate. Section 7 and the Manager column of the feature table are right cell by cell, and no blank cell is reachable by a manager through any RPC or policy: the single function `coaches_athlete` (coach only) keeps managers out of check-ins, injuries, game goals, clearances and attendance histories, and `coaches_group` keeps them out of session plans. What the audit found is one safeguarding gap in the database (A1), a records gap when an account is deleted (A2), a handful of small code and in-app text fixes (B), about fifteen wording slips, and a stale screenshot. The manager section itself is short, so section D lists what a manager can do that it never mentions.

## C. Wording slips (guide only)

1. Section 7 intro: a manager with a role but no placement still gets the Attendance card (whole squad or whole club sessions) and "You are not in a squad yet" under My squads and teams; worth one clause.
2. Section 7 "see and correct your own groups' sessions": managers also see every whole-club session and correct any session they took themselves, including Whole squad and Whole club ones (`20261007130000_attendance.sql:163-179, 185`).
3. Section 7 / section 6 "Sending messages", step 2 "the group is pre-selected if you coach just one": read as a manager, "coach or manage".
4. Section 6 "Sessions lists past sessions" (which section 7 points managers at): a manager's Attendance screen has no Sessions or Athletes tabs; the sessions list sits under the absence cards (`src/app/attendance/index.tsx:89-98`).
5. Section 6 "everyone must be marked before saving" applies to a new session; when correcting a session, athletes can be left Not marked (`save_attendance` vs `update_attendance`).
6. Section 6 volunteer declaration "says you're not paid for the role": true for the parent, under-18 and short-term visitor declarations; the national event declaration has no not-paid clause (`20261013060000_temporary_volunteers.sql:238-254`).
7. Section 6 "the app refuses a second [visitor or event exemption] within 12 months": it counts only an earlier exemption that was declared and had started, not one that was never accepted.
8. Section 12 / section 1 "need a current WWCC or exemption before they're approved": an open exemption the person hasn't yet declared is enough to approve; the role stays paused until they accept the declaration, and the home screen says "Your manager role is paused until you accept the exemption declaration" (`20261013055000_security_follow_ups.sql:213-224`).
9. Section 10 (admin, line 1624): "A coach's, manager's or physio's role pauses on the day their check expires" should be "the day after" (the card is current on its expiry date, `20261008000000_child_safety.sql:45-56`); section 12 already says "the day after". Fix now rather than wait for the admin audit.
10. Section 11 "Integrity training certificates … Coaches don't see them": coaches, managers and physios don't (`20261009120000_integrity_training.sql:133-146`).
11. Section 11 "Game goals and reflections": the list of who doesn't see them should name managers alongside the committee, physios and admins.
12. Section 11 "Your profile and squads … names and positions only" and "Preferred name and title: You and admins": a member's name and preferred name also show club-wide in the Raise a concern people picker (including to people not yet approved), on absences they reported and on the conduct record (`concern_people`, `absences.reported_by_name`, `conduct_acceptances.accepted_by_name`).
13. Section 11 "Live game stats": coaches and managers of the team see every game in every status and who paired, not only live ones.
14. (Withdrawn: section 1 already says anyone but a guest or temporary helper can also be a statistician.)
15. Section 1 "Absence reasons list" row means managing the list (admins); section 11 says everyone sees it. Rename the row "Manage absence reasons".
16. Section 3 "Delete account": say that the club keeps the member's code of conduct acceptances and, for coaches and managers, their season KPIs (decided, E2). Attendance marks go with the account (`attendance_marks.profile_id … on delete cascade`, `20261007130000_attendance.sql:40`), and concerns they raised stay as "A former member".
17. Section 6 parent-volunteer exemption, for managers: the manager must hold the Guardian role and be linked to the athlete first ("Link them as a guardian of that athlete first").

## D. Things a manager can do that the guide doesn't mention

Worth adding to section 7:
- The home screen header reads "Manager · Manager, Mixed Open Squad" (role, then the team title); while paused it reads "Manager (paused)" and the Messages, Attendance and My squads and teams cards disappear.
- Messages: a manager who is also on the committee gets the **Everyone** / **A squad or team** chooser; physios placed in the group get group messages too; the read count includes guardian copies; "Nobody else is in that group yet" when a group is empty.
- Attendance: "From today's calendar" chips (up to 6); the register shows who took the session, each absence's reason and "Injured, back {date}" whatever the consent; the Whole squad choice doesn't show the other squad's calendar.
- Season KPIs: **Clear** your own view, **Mark as not done**, up to 10 KPIs per group; the card and screen appear only once you're in a current squad or team ("You're not in a squad or team this season, so there are no KPIs to show"); a paused manager can still record progress.
- Awards: a manager can be a nominee in an **Any member** award, and the to-do reads "Vote for {award}" / "Vote in N awards"; the card says "N votes waiting for you".
- Raise a concern and, if named Child Safety Officer or Complaints Manager, the **Concerns** register card (any cleared adult can be named, `20261012235000_concerns.sql:46, 64`); the section 1 table has no row for either.
- Calendar: event **Notes** card; a dated (visitor or event) exemption with a future first day keeps the role paused until that day.
- A stand-down removes the manager's placements for the season and lifting it restores them.

Not worth adding: 4,000-character message limit; inbox and sent lists capped at 100; personal task titles capped at 200 characters; concern limit of 10 a day; no push notifications for messages.
