# Parent and guardian guide audit — 19 October 2026

Scope: the parent and guardian role guide (`docs/user-guide.md` section 5 in full, the Guardian row and column of the section 1 tables, sections 2 and 3 read as a guardian, every section 11 row that names parents or guardians, and section 12 "For parents and guardians" and "Who counts as a child"). Every claim was checked against main at `3197d32` (after PR #156): screens in `src/app`, logic in `src/lib`, and the database rules in `supabase/migrations`, always the last definition of each function. Who-sees claims were checked against the database rule, not only the screen. `is_guardian_of(athlete)` (a guardianship row, and the child under 18 by their checked date of birth) is the rule behind every guardian right; `is_verified_guardian_of` adds the admin's verification for consents, the code of conduct and the records list. The guide was last changed for this role in PR #141 (17 October) without a written report; this is that report.

**Result:** the consent gate, check-ins, game goals, team goals, injuries and documents, photo consent, the records list, What's changed, attendance and absences, session plans, season KPIs, survey copies, message copies, the code of conduct, integrity training, the safeguarding notices and the end of access at 18 all match the guide at the database. The audit found one regression that has been live since PR #143 merged this morning (A1: a guardian placed in their child's squad lost the squad's calendar, matches and roster), one guardian right that outlived the child's 18th birthday (A2: named survey answers), one guide claim that is true only before approval (A3: the 6-digit code), and one case where the app and the guide disagree about who answers a squad survey (A4: a guardian placed in the squad). A1 and A2 are fixed on this PR with database tests that fail on main; A3 and A4 are Matt's call.

## C. Wording slips (guide only, fixed on this PR)

1. Section 5, attendance: the list covers the last 4 weeks, 3 months or 12 months (a chip), not "every session"; a guardian can **Withdraw** an absence they or their child reported.
2. Section 5, records: a viewer with none of the listed roles (a manager reading a child's named survey answers) is listed as "member".
3. Section 5, consent: a consent a guardian gave stays in force under their name if they delete their account (`child_consents.given_by … on delete set null`, `given_by_name` kept), until another guardian withdraws it.
4. Section 5, calendars: say which squad or team a guardian sees (the one they're placed in, chosen at Join or added by an admin), and that after selections an admin has to add them to the child's team for its matches. Guardians aren't moved to teams at selections.

## D. Things a guardian can do that the guide doesn't mention

- Withdraw an absence (C1).
- See **Results for the team** on a shared poll from their child's squad (section 3 says so; the guardian's copy counts as a recipient).
- Their own integrity training courses (A5).
- A guardian placed in the squad gets the squad's messages once, with no child named (the guide says so); for surveys they now get the child-named copy (A4).
