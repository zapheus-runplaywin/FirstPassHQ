# Coach guide audit — 17 October 2026

Scope: the coach role guide (`docs/user-guide.md` section 6 in full, the Coach row and column of the section 1 tables, the coach-relevant rows of section 11, and the section 12 parts "Who counts as a child" and "For coaches, managers, physios and volunteers"). Sections 2 and 3, which the coach PDF also carries, were audited and fixed with the athlete guide (PR #140). Every claim was checked against main at `471de7a`: screens in `src/app`, logic in `src/lib`, and the database rules (RLS and functions) in `supabase/migrations`. Who-sees claims were checked against the database rule, not only the screen.

**Result:** the coach guide is accurate on the big things (who a coach looks after, clearances, attendance, session plans, KPIs). It has one safeguarding gap in the code (A1), two code bugs the guide exposes (B2, B3), three stale in-app texts (B1, B4, B5), and about two dozen wording slips. Nothing a coach is told they can do is missing from the app.

## C. Wording slips (guide only)

1. Coach row, section 1: a coach can also hold the short-term visitor or national event exemption, not only the parent and under-18 ones. Same for Manager and Physio rows.
2. Section 12: "Your role pauses the day your check expires" should be "the day after", matching section 6 ("once the expiry date has passed"); the card counts as current on its expiry date (`20261008000000_child_safety.sql:45-56`).
3. Volunteer declaration: the words are "no other state **or territory** has refused, **suspended** or cancelled a working with children clearance" (`20261012230000_volunteer_exemptions.sql:149-151`).
4. "You keep your squad and team places" while paused: true in the database, but they're hidden from the home screen (and the title) until the role comes back (`src/lib/auth.tsx:153-158`). Say so.
5. Combining roles: an athlete can't coach the squad they play in, any team named from it, or the squad a team they play in belongs to (`20261009110000_multiple_roles.sql:113-135`).
6. Section 12 "their parents and guardians can see who looked": only **verified** parents or guardians.
7. Sending messages: "Everyone in that squad or team gets it" except the sender; read counts are "of everyone else".
8. Athletes' check-ins: "Tap an athlete to see their last four weeks" works for athletes who checked in; the "Not checked in" names aren't tappable (`src/app/check-ins/index.tsx:190-199`). Either say so or make them tappable (a coach may want the history of someone who's gone quiet).
9. Match awards: the app never shows "Coach's MVP · Mixed Open A · Round 3 v Rockets" as one line. The award name is a bold line with "Mixed Open A · Round 3 v Rockets" beneath; the to-do reads "Vote for Coach's MVP" (or "Vote in N awards") (`src/lib/awards.ts:427-431`, `src/lib/todos.ts:295-316`).
10. Setting season KPIs: the buttons are **Edit KPI** / **Edit OKR**, then **Save**; **Remove KPI** / **Remove OKR** is on the edit screen and warns "Remove it, with its progress and result?" (`src/app/kpis/person.tsx:407-408`, `edit.tsx`).
11. Game goals (coach): add that opening an under-18's game goals is recorded for their parents, as the KPI paragraph says (`src/app/goals/game.tsx:71`).
12. Session plans: "Fill in from the calendar" offers the 8 sessions closest to today from the group's and the club's calendars within two weeks either side, not every session (`src/app/session-plans/edit.tsx:47, 574`).
13. Session plans: **Use again for another session** also works on plans shared with you, not only your own (`[id].tsx:190`), once B3 is fixed.
14. Session plans: adding a plan, being shared one and being told about one all need a current check or exemption (`session_plans.sql:134-150, 355-394`). One clause.
15. Taking attendance: in a club without squads the second choice is **Another team** (any current top-level team), not **Whole squad** (`src/app/attendance/new.tsx:159-166`).
16. Attendance records: "New or changed absences" sits under the Take attendance button, not "at the very top"; and anything you reported or withdrew yourself is excluded, not only your own child's.
17. Section 11, Live game stats: coaches and managers of the team see the game exists (its setup row) before pairing; the stats need the pairing code (`20261015000100_game_stats.sql:184-201`). Also, statisticians see fixtures (Match fixtures row).
18. Section 1 feature table has no row for reviewing absences (coaches, managers, admins), although section 3 and section 6 describe it.
19. Admin reminders: parent and under-18 exemptions ending within 60 days also remind admins, not only cards (`20261016020000_wwcc_todo_people.sql:65-70`).

## D. Things a coach can do that the guide doesn't mention

Worth adding:
- Trends: tap a day to see that day's numbers in every chart; the chip row switches group in Day by day.
- Injury screen: full history with physio updates (documents viewable), "Raise a concern" link, and opening an injury is itself recorded (separately from documents).
- Taking attendance: the group is pre-selected if you lead only one; everyone must be marked before saving; the session name can be edited after prefill and when changing a session; athletes who joined since show as "Not marked".
- Game goals: played games show "N goals · k of N reflected · n coach comments" with Load more; a coach can edit or remove a goal set by another coach; a team goal review can be updated later.
- Season KPIs: "Details (optional)", "Note on the result", **Clear result**, up to 10 per person per group, and a coach can remove the person's own view at a review.
- Session plans: Today / Yesterday / Tomorrow picks, optional start and finish times and place, "Leave out" to drop a file before saving, and a plan shows "Not shared. Only you and the club's admins can see it" until shared.
- Staff titles combine: "Head Coach, Open A and Open", or "Head Coach, Open A · Manager, 17 & Under".

Not worth adding: 4,000-character message limit; sessions list capped at 200; share-by-name capped at 50 coaches; Take a photo absent on the web.
