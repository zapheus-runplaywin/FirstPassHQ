# Admin guide audit — 19 October 2026

Scope: the admin role guide (`docs/user-guide.md` section 10 in full, the Admin row and column of the section 1 tables, every section 11 row that names admins, and section 12 "For committees, boards and admins" and "For the governing body and auditors"). Every claim was checked against main at `73a604a` (after PR #160): screens in `src/app`, logic in `src/lib`, and the database rules in `supabase/migrations`, always the last definition of each function. Who-sees claims were checked against the database rule (`is_admin()`, the RLS policies and each `admin_*` function), not only the screen. Matt asked (19 October) to be checked with wherever the guide and the app disagree, or the role's intent looks misaligned with what was built, before anything is changed; so this report proposes and nothing is built yet.

**Result:** the admin role is the best-matched of the nine. Approvals, verification, roles and titles, Working With Children Checks, exemptions, notices and stand-downs, seasons, awards, fixtures, calendars, absence reasons, club captains, role messages, integrity training, KPI reviews, the injury report, session plans, every report, club settings, every keep period and its default, safeguarding contacts, the concerns register, the club password, affiliation and the PlayHQ registration checks all match the guide at the database. The audit found one data-losing behaviour the guide doesn't warn about (A1: naming a team from a squad wipes the team's guardian and physio placements), one place where the guide disagrees with itself and the app sits in between (A2: admins and team goals), one feature switch that doesn't hide its Administration page (A3), one keep-period claim that reads as always-on but only runs with **Remove old records** on (A4), and a set of smaller wording gaps (sections B and C). A1 and A2 are Matt's call; the rest are proposed as small code fixes (B) or guide fixes (C).

## C. Wording slips (guide only, proposed)

1. **Section 10 intro (to-do list):** the list of child-safety to-dos leaves out several that `admin_safety_todos` raises and that section 12 names: athletes with no date of birth, Screening Unit notices to report to the governing body, no WWC Registration Officer, no cleared Child Safety Officer or Complaints Manager, and new concerns. When Affiliation records are on it also leaves out members not registered in PlayHQ. (`20261018230000_role_guide_audits.sql`, `admin_safety_todos`; `src/lib/todos.ts`.)
2. **Section 10, Seasons:** "Archive the old season once it has finished" — the database refuses until the next season has been started ("Start the next season before archiving this one"), which is why the steps are in that order. Say so. (`20261009000000_optional_squads.sql:415-432`.)
3. **Section 10, Approving members, "No team or role for 2 years":** the list also leaves out temporary helpers (and, as it says, committee, admins and the Head Physio). (`20261017120000_retention_gaps.sql:255-293`.)
4. **Section 10, Integrity training:** "Copy 2026–27 training: each course is copied" — the button appears only while last season has a non-archived course whose name this season lacks; archived courses and courses already in the season by name are never copied, and groups are matched by name **and** kind (a squad doesn't match a team of the same name). **Archive** and **Remove** are on the Change screen, and an archived course can be brought back. (`src/app/admin/training/index.tsx:35-41, 89-103`; `20261009120000_integrity_training.sql:395-416`; `src/app/admin/training/edit.tsx:375-393`.)
5. **Section 10, Integrity training:** "only members in those groups with a chosen role must complete it" — when squads or teams are chosen, the course reaches people placed in those groups as athletes, coaches, managers or physios only; **Guardians**, **Committee** and **Admins** chosen with particular groups reach nobody (the committee audit's finding 21 said this for the committee; it is the same for guardians and admins). Say that those three roles need **All squads and teams**. (`training_applies`, `20261009120000_integrity_training.sql:117-129`.)
6. **Section 10, Season KPI reviews:** "A date can be removed only while no reviews have been written for it" — it is also blocked once anyone has confirmed or self-reviewed against it, or a reviewer saved and then cleared a note. "once anyone has written or confirmed a review for it". The reminder to-do goes to the person who set the KPIs only while they can still edit them (still committee or admin, or still that group's coach, and the season not archived). Admins see an athlete under Season KPIs only once their coaches have set something. (`20261016000000_season_kpis.sql:804-822`; `20261018220000_committee_guide_audit.sql:286-293, 313-324`.)
7. **Section 10, Injury report:** "Export CSV saves the same list" — the CSV has more columns than the PDF: email, left club, cleared on, and the number of updates and documents. Mention the email addresses, since the PDF warning about personal details covers the CSV too. (`src/app/admin/injuries.tsx:251-283`.)
8. **Section 10, Session plans for admins:** **Delete this plan** first removes the plan's files from storage and then the plan; if a file is left behind that the plan never recorded (an upload that failed half-way), the app says "The plan's files are still there. Try deleting it again" each time. Rare; worth one sentence, or a later fix that removes stray files. (`src/lib/session-plans.ts:246-262, 307-316`; `20261016010000_session_plans.sql:701-719`.)
9. **Section 10, Reports, Working With Children Checks:** there is a **Current** section between **Needs attention** and the exempt committee members and admins; and **Exempt from a check** leaves out a current exemption whose member isn't approved (ended ones are always listed). (`src/lib/reports.ts:264-324`.)
10. **Section 10, Reports, Attendance:** "For chosen squads and teams it counts the athletes on their registers" — it counts the athletes marked on one of those groups' registers in the period, not the squad or team's members: someone with no mark in the period isn't counted, someone marked at a session who isn't placed in the group is. (`src/app/admin/reports/attendance.tsx:159-161`.)
11. **Section 10, Reports, Session plans:** the position column can also read "Coach" (a coach with no title). (`20261016010000_session_plans.sql:565-570`.)
12. **Section 10, Reports, Season KPIs:** a coach or manager who deleted their account stays on the report only if they had at least one KPI in that group. (`season_kpi_report`, `20261018220000_committee_guide_audit.sql`.)
13. **Section 10, Reports, Integrity training:** archived courses are left out. (`src/lib/report-data.ts:73`.)
14. **Section 10, Reports, Award results:** "how many people voted" — a match award's standings give the number of ballots over all matches; distinct voters show per match. (`src/lib/reports.ts:977-981, 1023-1026`.)
15. **Section 10, Club settings, Features:** the list omits **Season KPIs**, the twelfth switch in the same list on screen (the guide covers it in its own bullet, which reads as a separate section). The screen's order is Squads, Features, Affiliation records, Physios get club messages. (`src/lib/club.tsx:39-44`; `src/app/admin/club.tsx:319-396`.)
16. **Section 10, Keeping records:** "each night" is a GitHub Actions schedule (2:17 am Perth), which GitHub pauses in a repository with no activity for 60 days. (`.github/workflows/purge-expired-injuries.yml:10-17`.)
17. **Section 10, Safeguarding contacts:** the WWC Registration Officer may also be an admin or committee member covered by a volunteer exemption, like the other two contacts; none of the three can be a guest or temporary helper; and a contact whose check lapses or who is stood down comes back by themselves when the check is recorded or the stand-down lifted, without being chosen again. "Committee members approved before checks were needed keep their role without one until then" applies to any committee member without a card, whenever approved. (`20261013030000_wwcc_records.sql:200-209`; `20261018230000_role_guide_audits.sql:14-27`; `20261012235000_concerns.sql:64-118`; `20261015000100_game_stats.sql:58-66`.)
18. **Section 10, The concerns register:** the red referral note also goes once a concern is closed; the note on a referral is optional; and **Export the register as a PDF** leaves out any concern about the person exporting it (section 12 says this, the register section doesn't). (`src/app/concerns/[id].tsx:118`; `20261012235000_concerns.sql:455-470`; `src/app/concerns/report.tsx:22-36`.)
19. **Section 10, Affiliation:** "The app records who sent it, when and where" — the record is made when the share sheet closes (or the download starts on the web), whether or not the email went, and "where" is the return address the club entered. **PlayHQ registrations** lists approved members in the newest season that isn't archived. (`src/components/report-view.tsx:31-39`; `20261013093000_affiliation.sql:72-85, 296-331`.)
20. **Section 10, Awards:** "only the name, description, closing dates, match voting window and listed names can change" — the **Preseason share** stays fixed too (it is in the list that is refused), while whether the award is annual can change. Accurate enough; no change needed unless the annual flag is ever shown.

## D. Things an admin can do that the guide doesn't mention

- **Name a team** also lists the squad's physios, with **In this team** / **Not in this team** (section 10 names coaches and managers only).
- **Messages to a role** refuses Guest and Temporary helper, since they get no messages; the picker doesn't offer them. (`send_group_message`.)
- **Calendars:** a committee calendar is seen by committee members and admins only; a squad or team calendar by its members (athletes, guardians placed in it, and coaches, managers and physios with a current check). (`20261007040000_committee_calendars.sql:27`.)
- **Live game stats:** admins count as statisticians for every game (`is_statistician()` includes admins), which is why section 11's row is right.
